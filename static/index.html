Panorama

Take a 360° panorama from your telescope spot (e.g. phone pano mode), upload it, calibrate, then paint over trees/houses.

Calibration

FOV < 360° for partial panoramas. Azimuths outside the image are treated as completely unblocked.

Crop (sides only)

Drag horizontally to select full-height bands to remove from the left and/or right edge — e.g. trim a 370° pano back to 360°. Cropping resets paint mask and profile.
