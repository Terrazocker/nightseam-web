Features

A planner that knows the night sky

Everything is computed locally in your browser — the catalog, the sky and your data never leave the page.

🌙 Tonight’s picks

Ranked targets with clear-window times, moon-aware scoring and a Bortle-adjusted view of what the night can deliver.

🗺 Live polar sky map

Zoom, pan and scrub the all-sky chart with the sun, moon and 12,000 objects — a rectangular window that shows you more.

◆ S–D target grades

Every object graded for visual impact — brightness, size, fame and type — so the showpieces are always easy to spot.

🛰 Horizon panoramas

Import a 360° panorama, paint the trees and houses, and see which targets your skyline actually lets you see.

📕 Sites, rigs & timezones

Your observing locations and equipment — per-site timezones, DST-aware, backed up as JSON.

💻 100% client-side

The entire catalog (Messier + OpenNGC) ships with the page — works offline, nothing uploaded, no account.

⛅ Weather

Current conditions and a 4-day forecast for your observing site — loaded on demand, nothing fetched until you click.

🔎 Rectangular sky zoom

Wheel-zoom opens a rectangular window over the all-sky chart — more sky than a circular magnifier, with drag-pan and double-click reset.
