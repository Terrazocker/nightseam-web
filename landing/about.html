About NightSeam

A free, open project for planning deep-sky nights and keeping the images.

How the planner works

  • All astronomy runs in your browser: positions, twilight and visibility windows.
  • Your sites, rigs and horizons stay in browser storage. No account, no tracking.
  • Weather and object photos load from online services. See Privacy.

Sources and credits

  • Catalog: 12,176 catalog objects from OpenNGC by Mattia Verga (CC BY-SA 4.0), plus a small supplement.
  • Photos: Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Each photo shows its author and license. Image credits
