About

What is NightSeam?

A deep-sky night-planning app. The web version runs entirely in your browser; the Windows desktop version adds the full Image Catalog for organizing your FITS captures.

How it works

All calculations run on your device using standard astronomy algorithms — no internet service is involved. Sun, moon and twilight times follow the classic Meeus formulas, and object positions are accurate to well under a tenth of a degree.

Data, privacy & credits

  • Catalog: Messier + OpenNGC (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • Images: Wikimedia Commons, each image under its own license.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo, loaded on demand; weather data © Open-Meteo.com.
  • Your data: stays on your device — in the browser's local storage or in a data folder next to the desktop app. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.
