About

What is AstroPilot?

A deep-sky night-planning app. The web version runs entirely in your browser; the Windows desktop version adds the full Image Catalog for organizing your FITS captures.

Where do the numbers come from?

All sky calculations are computed locally from standard published algorithms — no internet service involved: sunrise/sunset/twilight from the Sun's geocentric position (Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms), moon phase from the Moon-Sun elongation, object altitudes from J2000 catalog coordinates precessed to date with sidereal time and refraction. Typical errors are well below 0.1° for stars and deep-sky objects.
