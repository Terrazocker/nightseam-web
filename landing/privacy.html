Privacy Policy

The short version

NightSeam is designed to keep your data on your own device. Neither this website nor the web planner stores anything about you on a server. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies beyond what your browser needs to run the page.

Web version (planner)

  • Local storage: your observing sites, rigs and custom targets are stored only in your browser's local data (IndexedDB / localStorage) on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Object catalog & popularity data: the object list is derived from OpenNGC (CC BY-SA 4.0) and ships with the page. The community popularity counts behind the ranking come from a the popularity source snapshot taken once, when the catalog is built; the counts are baked into the catalog file, so opening the planner does not contact the popularity source or any other server.
  • Weather: when you load the weather panel, your active site's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch a forecast. No other data is transmitted. Weather data © Open-Meteo.com.
  • Location: the "use my location" button uses your browser's geolocation API — it is only queried when you press the button, and the coordinates are stored locally like any other site.
  • Object images: example photos are either bundled with the page or fetched from Wikimedia Commons when you open an object's detail view.

Hosting / infrastructure

The site is served via a hosting provider and a CDN, which process standard technical data (IP address, user agent, request log) to deliver the pages. We do not use this data for profiling, and we do not combine it with any other data sources.

Your rights

You are in control of the data on your device: delete the storage in your browser to remove your saved sites, equipment and panoramas. If you have questions, use the contact details from the Imprint.
