Options

When on, the app fetches weather for the active site every time it opens, so the chip is already filled.

Display

Takes that much space away on each side of the content. 0 lets it fill the window, higher values center a narrower column.

Scrub across the whole 24 h day. Off (default) keeps the scrub on the night itself — sunset to sunrise.

Advanced

Standard mode shows the 200 most popular targets; advanced adds the full OpenNGC catalog — NGC/IC plus its addendum and a 16-object supplement OpenNGC cannot carry (12,176 objects to magnitude 20). Changing this reloads the app.

Plan and display the night in this zone (only in advanced mode). Empty = the active site's zone, or this browser's zone. DST is applied per date.

Ranks targets down while the Moon is up; objects far from the Moon and clusters survive moonlight best.

1 = pristine dark sky, 9 = city centre. Darker skies lower the default minimum altitude and weaken moonlight penalties.

Objects you own filters for are marked in the lists.
